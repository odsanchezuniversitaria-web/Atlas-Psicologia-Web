Atlas browser compatibility

The native LiveKit Unity runtime and its optional UniTask assembly exclude
the WebGL player platform in their assembly definitions. Editor and native
platforms retain the upstream SDK. The browser player uses the separate
livekit-client JavaScript bundle in Assets/WebGLTemplates/AtlasNexus.

This avoids invoking the native livekit_ffi initialization on a browser.
