Atlas browser compatibility

MRUK.Shared.InitializeSharedLibrary and MRUKGlobalContext.CreateInstance
are not registered as RuntimeInitializeOnLoad callbacks on WebGL players.
Their native/editor behavior is retained. The Atlas Web scenes use WebXR
and do not use the native MRUK shared library.

The upstream unconditional callbacks attempted to invoke native function
pointers on WebGL and stopped the player before the first scene loaded.
